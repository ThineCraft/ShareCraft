Target date about 30 Nov 2026. Branch s3-surfacing, worktree ShareCraft-s3. Plan: plans/2026-11-18-s3-surfacing.html. Nothing built yet under this proposal.

What the demo shows

An administrator approves the Cloudflare R2 endpoint. A site manager makes a new library "From S3 storage" with a bucket, an optional prefix and an access key (typed by the owner into the page, stored only encrypted). The bucket's files appear as folders and files; people open, preview and download them (bytes fetched on first open, scanned, cached). A file added to the bucket in the R2 dashboard appears after the next poll or after "Update now". Read-only.

Costs

R2 free tier per month: 10 GB stored, 1 million list/write operations (class A), 10 million read operations (class B), no charge for data out. A demo bucket of a few MB polled every 5 minutes uses about 9,000 list operations a month: no charge. Over the free tier: $4.50 per million class A, $0.36 per million class B, $0.015 per GB-month. Because Task 12 (the cap) is deferred, leave the demo library disconnected after the demo or keep the bucket small.
