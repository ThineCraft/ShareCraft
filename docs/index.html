The proposition

Built to remain understandable—and yours.

ShareCraft is intended to become the shared-content foundation of the ecosystem: a place for organizational knowledge to be published, governed, searched, and reused without being trapped.

Proposed scope

What ShareCraft is intended to make possible.

01

Planned capability

Sites and structured knowledge

Team sites, communication sites, intranets, pages, news, lists, navigation, and reusable content are part of the intended SharePoint-alternative scope.

02

Planned capability

Document libraries and governance

Document libraries are intended to combine metadata, versions, search, permissions, sharing controls, auditability, retention, and governance.

03

Planned capability

Co-authoring across the craft

Presence and co-authoring are intended for WordCraft, GridCraft, PdfCraft, SolidCraft, DeckCraft, and other compatible ThineCraft applications.

04

Planned capability

Workflows and a controlled public edge

Workflow integration is intended to connect content processes across the ecosystem, while controlled public submissions can receive records without exposing the private workspace.

One connected craft

Continue through the ecosystem.

Open tools. Independent infrastructure. ThineCraft

A planned family of open tools for creating, collaborating, and retaining ownership of organizational knowledge.

↗ Sync, migration, and continuity DriveCraft

A planned hybrid OneDrive alternative spanning desktop synchronization, managed metadata, migration, and organization-controlled offline copies.

↗ Communication without captivity TeamCraft

A planned open communication and meetings platform for teams that want portable conversations and records.

↗ Plans that remain legible GanttCraft

A planned project scheduling and resource-management system centered on understandable plans and durable data.

↗ Automation you can inspect FlowCraft

A planned open workflow and integration platform for connecting ThineCraft services and external systems.

↗ Make systems understandable ShowCraft

A planned environment for diagrams, animation, business intelligence, and digital twins.

↗ Paper and digital, one record FormCraft

A planned forms platform connecting browser entry, fillable PDFs, guided paper capture, and verifiable records.

↗ Your infrastructure, one control plane CloudCraft

A planned Rust-based distributed platform for self-hosted workloads, open-source applications, backups, and disaster recovery.
