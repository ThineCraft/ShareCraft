Sites, libraries, and collaboration

A SharePoint alternative shaped around ownership.

Open-source team sites, document libraries, and sharing, written in Rust. Your files are kept as Git revisions on servers you control, and spreadsheets, documents, PDFs and 3D designs open right in the browser.

Early development build Running and tested, but not a release yet. The screenshots below are from the real app.

Why ShareCraft

Built to remain understandable—and yours.

Shared sites, libraries and real collaboration usually come with a corporate licence and someone else's servers. ShareCraft is meant to be the shared-content foundation of ThineCraft: a place where knowledge is published, governed and reused without being trapped, and where anyone who needs those tools can have them.

Not only for companies

For anyone who has to come together.

01

Families

When a crisis arrives

An illness, a death, a house to clear, a parent who needs care. Suddenly a family spread across towns has to share documents, decisions and news, often with people they rarely speak to. They deserve the same tools a company has, kept private to the family.

02

Communities

Clubs, charities and groups

Volunteers come and go, but the records have to stay. A site with clear permissions means the next treasurer or organiser finds everything where it should be.

03

Organisations

Teams that want to own their work

Intranets, project sites and document libraries on servers you run, with history you can read and no licence that decides when you have to leave.

See it working

What ShareCraft does today.

Every picture is the working app, showing a made-up architecture firm.

01

Sites and document libraries

Each team gets a site with its own libraries and folders. Upload, rename, move, copy and delete files; deleted items go to a recycle bin, and quotas keep storage in check. A library of 100,000 files has been measured.

02

Open files in the browser

Spreadsheets open in GridCraft, word documents in WordCraft, PDFs in PdfCraft, and 3D designs in SolidCraft, all inside ShareCraft. Every save becomes a revision. For now, one person edits a file at a time while others read; editing a spreadsheet together is being built.

03

Photo libraries

An image library can show pictures as a grid of thumbnails, for project photos, models and renders.

04

Sharing you can understand

See who has access to any site, folder or file, and where that access comes from. Give access to people or groups, make sharing links, answer access requests, and break or restore inheritance. The explorer shows the whole permission tree at once.

05

People and groups

Administrators add accounts, disable them, and organise people into groups. Someone whose access is removed is put out of any open document at once.

06

Your look, light or dark

Each site can choose a theme and colours, with a live preview before saving. Every page follows the reader's light or dark setting.

07

Works on a phone

The same pages fit a small screen, with a bottom bar for moving between sites, files and people.

Honest status

Built, and still to come.

Working now

  • Accounts, groups and roles: viewer, editor, owner
  • Team sites, navigation and document and image libraries
  • Uploads, recycle bin, copy and storage quotas
  • Sharing links, access requests and permission inheritance
  • GridCraft, WordCraft, PdfCraft and SolidCraft in the browser
  • Plain-text documents edited together, live
  • Every save kept as a Git revision; records in PostgreSQL
  • Site themes and branding, light and dark
  • Production mode behind your own HTTPS reverse proxy

Not built yet

  • Editing a spreadsheet together, live (in progress)
  • Libraries on Windows file shares and S3 storage (partly built)
  • Lists, pages and news
  • Search
  • Workflows
  • Guests and notifications

One connected craft

Continue through the ecosystem.

Open tools. Independent infrastructure. ThineCraft

A planned family of open tools for creating, collaborating, and retaining ownership of organizational knowledge.

↗ Sync, migration, and continuity DriveCraft

A planned hybrid OneDrive alternative spanning desktop synchronization, managed metadata, migration, and organization-controlled offline copies.

↗ Communication without captivity TeamCraft

A planned open communication and meetings platform for teams that want portable conversations and records.

↗ Plans that remain legible GanttCraft

A planned project scheduling and resource-management system centered on understandable plans and durable data.

↗ Automation you can inspect FlowCraft

A planned open workflow and integration platform for connecting ThineCraft services and external systems.

↗ Make systems understandable ShowCraft

A planned environment for diagrams, animation, business intelligence, and digital twins.

↗ Paper and digital, one record FormCraft

A planned forms platform connecting browser entry, fillable PDFs, guided paper capture, and verifiable records.

↗ Your infrastructure, one control plane CloudCraft

A planned Rust-based distributed platform for self-hosted workloads, open-source applications, backups, and disaster recovery.
