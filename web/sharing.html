Organisation settings

Sharing

When links for anyone are on, a person without an account can view and download what such a link shares, and anyone who has the address can use it. Turn them on only if you need them. Turning them off stops every link for anyone at once.

Applies to links made from now on. Lowering it does not shorten links that already exist: they last until their own expiry unless revoked.

Language and time zone

Pages outside a site, and new sites, use these. A site keeps its own once it is set.

Blocked file types

No library takes these types, whatever its own settings. Files already stored stay, and are marked.

Separated by commas, like .exe, .bat. Leave it empty to block none.

    Malware scanner

    Every new upload is sent to the organisation's own scanner over ICAP, and nobody can open it until the scanner has passed it. With no scanner, files are available at once.

    Like icap://scanner.example.org/avscan (port 1344 unless given). Leave it empty for no scanner.

    Files stored before scanning

    Files stored before the scanner was set up are not scanned unless you ask. They stay available while they wait.
